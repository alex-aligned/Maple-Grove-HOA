Community Rules & Standards

The shared agreements that keep Maple Grove a great place to live. Last updated March 2026.

Dogs must be leashed at all times outside your unit, including walkways, the clubhouse lawn, and around the pool. Owners are responsible for cleaning up after pets — waste stations are at the corner of Maple Lane and near the mailboxes.

Maximum pets per unit: 2 dogs or 3 cats, or a reasonable combination.

Updated Mar 2026

Each unit is assigned 2 covered spots. Additional vehicles park in unmarked guest spaces (max 7 days/month). Guest passes for longer stays are available in the portal.

Not allowed in any common parking: RVs, boats, trailers, or commercial vehicles over 1 ton. Brief loading/unloading is fine.

Updated Jan 2026

From 10pm to 7am, please keep music, voices, and appliance noise to a level that wouldn't carry into a neighboring unit. On weekends, quiet hours start at 11pm.

Hosting a gathering? Let immediate neighbors know in advance — there's even a "letting neighbors know" template in the portal.

Updated Mar 2026

Any change visible from outside your unit — paint colors, fence repairs, solar panels, satellite dishes, awnings, landscaping replacements — needs board sign-off before work starts. Most requests are approved within 2 weeks.

What doesn't need approval: like-for-like maintenance, interior changes, planters, holiday decorations (up to 30 days).

Updated Feb 2026

The pool is open 6am – 9pm daily during summer (May 15 – Sept 30), 8am – 8pm otherwise. Children under 14 must be supervised. Glass containers are not allowed in the pool area.

Guests: Up to 4 guests per unit at a time. For larger gatherings, book the Pool Cabana or BBQ Patio through the Amenities page.

Updated May 2026

Trash: collected Tuesdays and Fridays. Recycling: Wednesdays. Compost: Mondays. Bins out after 6pm the night before, back in by 8pm the day of collection.

Bulk items: arrange a pickup through the portal (free, once per quarter). Don't leave large items by the dumpsters.

Updated Oct 2025

Short-term rentals (under 6 months, including Airbnb/VRBO) are not permitted. All long-term rentals must be registered with the HOA within 30 days of the lease start.

Owners remain responsible for tenant compliance with all community rules.

Updated Feb 2026
